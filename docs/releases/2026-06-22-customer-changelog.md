# What's New in Zebri

_Release: June 2026_

We've shipped one of our biggest updates yet. Zebri now does more of the
repetitive work for you, lets you send beautiful on-brand emails in seconds, and
gives your couples a friendlier place to plan. Here's everything that's new.

---

## ✨ Automations — put your follow-ups on autopilot

The headline feature. You can now build workflows that run by themselves, so the
work that happens for *every* couple no longer needs you to remember it.

Set up a flow once, for example:

> When a **quote is sent** → wait **2 days** → **email the couple** a friendly
> nudge → if they still haven't replied, **create a task** for you to call them.

**What you can build with:**

- **Triggers** (what kicks things off): a new enquiry arrives, a couple moves to
  a new stage, a quote is sent / accepted / due / overdue, an invoice is sent /
  paid / due / overdue, a contract is sent / signed, an event is coming up (e.g.
  "2 weeks before the wedding"), the wedding anniversary, a couple finishing a
  section of their portal, a task going overdue, and more.
- **Actions** (what happens): send an email, update a couple's stage, add a note,
  create or update a task, send a quote / invoice / contract, send a payment
  reminder, share the portal or run sheet, request information from the couple,
  send a thank-you or review request, and more.
- **Smart flow control:** add a **wait**, branch on an **if/else** condition, or
  **stop** the flow when it's no longer needed.

**Thoughtful touches built in:**

- **Quiet hours** — automated emails won't go out in the middle of the night.
  They wait for a sensible time.
- **Test before you trust it** — run any automation in test mode and the email
  comes to *your* inbox (marked `[Test]`) so you can see exactly what the couple
  would receive.
- **Run it manually** any time from a couple's profile.
- **Full visibility** — every couple now has an **Automations** tab showing what
  ran, what's waiting, and anything that needs attention, with one-click
  **Retry**, **Cancel**, and **Pause**.

Find it in the new **Automations** item in your sidebar.

## 📧 Email templates & sending — write it once, send it in seconds

Stop rewriting the same emails. Zebri now has a full email toolkit.

- **Email templates** — create reusable emails for every stage (enquiry replies,
  booking confirmations, payment reminders, pre-event coordination, post-event
  thank-yous).
- **Merge fields** — drop in things like the couple's name, the event date, or a
  quote link, and Zebri fills them in automatically for each couple.
- **A starter library** — don't start from a blank page. Browse a set of
  professionally written templates and add the ones you like with one click, then
  tweak them to sound like you.
- **Send straight from a couple's profile** — pick a template, see a **live
  preview** of exactly what they'll get, and send. Every couple now has an
  **Emails** tab with a full history of what you've sent.
- **Test send** — fire a copy to your own inbox first to be sure it looks right.

Find templates in the new **Templates** item in your sidebar.

## ✍️ Email signatures — your sign-off, done properly

Build a polished email signature once and it drops into any template.

- Rich formatting: **bold**, colours, links, and even an **image or logo**.
- Saves automatically as you go — no Save button to remember.

Set it up in **Settings → Signature**.

---

## 📨 Send from your own email address

Couples now see emails coming from *you*, not a generic address.

- Connect your own **Gmail or Outlook** so your emails send from your address,
  land in your **Sent** folder, and replies come straight back to your inbox.
- Prefer zero setup? Keep using Zebri's built-in sending — it works instantly.

Choose your option in **Settings → Public Page → Email**.

---

## 💍 Vows — a private, safe place for the words that matter

- Each partner can write their vows in their **own private space** in the portal.
  Partners can't see each other's until the day.
- As the MC, you can **edit** vows, and every change is saved with a **full
  history** — so you can always roll back to an earlier version (or the couple's
  original) without losing a thing.
- **Download a clean, printable PDF** of each set of vows for the ceremony.

---

## 💑 A friendlier client portal

Your couples' portal got a refresh that makes it feel like a shared planning
tool, not just a read-only page.

- **Couples can keep their own details up to date** — name, email and phone for
  both partners, saved automatically.
- **An interactive events timeline** — couples can add events and update the date
  or venue themselves, with a handy countdown to the big day. (You stay in
  control of event status.)
- **A personal link for each partner** — every partner gets their own private
  link, which keeps their vows separate and private.

---

## 🎨 Customise your couples pipeline

Make the Couples board match *your* workflow.

- **Add, rename, recolour, and reorder** the statuses on your board.
- Changes flow straight through to your board columns and filters.

Open it from the small gear icon on the **Couples** page.
