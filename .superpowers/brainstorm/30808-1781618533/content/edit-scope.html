Whose contact details can a partner edit?

Each partner has their own private link (primary vs spouse). This decides what the Overview lets them change.

A

Edit your own, see your partner's

The link's owner edits their name / email / phone. Their partner's details show as read-only (or "not added yet"). Mirrors the per-partner privacy model you built for vows.
